## Books

1. Jideofor Patrick C. Adibe, *[What Does Russia Really Want from Africa? Interrogating Russia's Re-Awakened Interest in Africa](https://www.adonis-abbey.com/book_detail.php?bookid=298)*, Adonis & Abbey Publishers Ltd., 2025.

2. David R. Black and David J. Hornsby (Eds.), *[South African Foreign Policy: Identities, Intentions, and Directions](https://www.routledge.com/South-African-Foreign-Policy-Identities-Intentions-and-Directions/Black-Hornsby/p/book/9780367030094)*, Routledge, 2017.

3. Peter Cunliffe-Jones, *[Fake News – What's the Harm?](https://www.fulcrum.org/concern/monographs/3197xq21t?locale=en)*, University of Westminster Press, 2025.

4. Admire Mare and Allen Munoriyarwa, *[Disinformation Campaigns in Africa: Actors, Consequences, and Responses](https://www.bloomsbury.com/us/disinformation-campaigns-in-africa-9781666945331/)*, Bloomsbury Academic, 2026.

5. Phiwokuhle Mnyandu, *[South Africa–China Relations: Between Aspiration and Reality in a New Global Order](https://www.bloomsbury.com/us/south-africachina-relations-9781793644527/)*, Lexington Books, 2021.

6. Phuthuma Nhleko, *[The Invisible People: How a Quarter of Humanity Can Thrive in Africa by 2050](https://www.penguinrandomhouse.co.za/product/invisible-people-9278811)*, Penguin Books, 2026.

7. Samuel Ramani, *[Russia in Africa: Resurgent Great Power or Bellicose Pretender?](https://global.oup.com/academic/product/russia-in-africa-9780197782538)*, Oxford University Press, 2023.

8. Tony Roberts and George Hamandishe Karekwaivanane (Eds.), *[Digital Disinformation in Africa: Hashtag Politics, Power and Propaganda](https://www.bloomsbury.com/us/digital-disinformation-in-africa-9781350319202/)*, Zed, Bloomsbury Publishing plc, 2024.

9. David H. Shinn and Joshua Eisenman, *[China's Relations with Africa: A New Era of Strategic Engagement](https://cup.columbia.edu/book/chinas-relations-with-africa/9780231558228)*, Columbia University Press, 2023.

10. Alphonce Shiundu, "Disinformation in Africa: Overview and Responses", in Lisa Ginsborg and Paula Gori (Eds.), *[Disinformation](https://www.springerprofessional.de/en/disinformation-in-africa-overview-and-responses/51931236)*, Springer Nature Switzerland, 2026.

11. Leonard Thompson, *[A History of South Africa](https://yalebooks.yale.edu/book/9780300189353/a-history-of-south-africa-fourth-edition)*, Fourth Edition, Yale University Press, 2014.

12. Herman Wasserman and Dani Madrid-Morales (Eds.), *[Disinformation in the Global South](https://www.wiley.com/en-us/shop/general-communication-media-studies/disinformation-in-the-global-south-p-9781119714446)*, John Wiley & Sons, 2022.
